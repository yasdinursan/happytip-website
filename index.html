What is HappyTip?

A simpler way to recognize value.

Tokenomics

A clearly defined 1 billion HTP supply.

Buyback Reserve

Liquidity

Supporting an accessible HTP market.

20% of the total supply, equal to 200,000,000 HTP, is allocated for liquidity provision. The purpose is to support an accessible and efficient market for HTP. The selected trading pair, deployment strategy and liquidity lock conditions will be disclosed before launch.

Burn Mechanism

Designed to reduce circulating supply over time.

HappyTip is designed with a supply-reduction mechanism intended to permanently remove eligible HTP from circulation. Final burn parameters will be disclosed before activation and made independently verifiable on-chain.

🔥

Permanent removal

When the burn mechanism is triggered, designated HTP is sent to an irrecoverable burn address. Burned tokens cannot be recovered, redistributed or returned to the market. This creates a transparent relationship between eligible ecosystem activity and long-term supply reduction.

TransparentBurn transactions can be independently verified through public blockchain data once the mechanism is active.

Distribution & Vesting

Designed for responsible token distribution.

Token allocation and release schedules should be transparent before tokens enter circulation. HappyTip separates allocation from vesting so the community can clearly distinguish where HTP is allocated and when it becomes available.

TGE

Token Generation Event

The TGE marks the point at which HTP distribution and initial circulating supply are activated according to the officially disclosed launch structure.

01

Cliff

Any applicable cliff period will be publicly disclosed for the relevant allocation before the associated tokens become eligible for release.

02

Vesting

Where vesting applies, tokens can be released according to a predefined schedule rather than becoming immediately available. Exact schedules will be published with the official allocation details.

03

Airdrop

Airdrop eligibility, snapshot rules, claim timing and allocation conditions will be announced separately. No unannounced airdrop amount is assumed here.

TBA

Release Schedule

Exact TGE, cliff and vesting percentages should be published before launch. This page intentionally avoids presenting unconfirmed release numbers as official facts.
