FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Key information about $HTP, HappyTip and the ecosystem.

What is HappyTip?

HappyTip is a social tipping ecosystem built around digital appreciation, community interaction and $HTP.

What is $HTP?

$HTP is the native utility token of the HappyTip ecosystem, supporting tipping, community participation and ecosystem utility.

What is the total $HTP supply?

$HTP has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens.

What is the Buyback Reserve?

10% of the total supply, equal to 100,000,000 $HTP, is designated for the ecosystem buyback reserve.

Will $HTP have a burn mechanism?

A permanent burn mechanism is planned as part of the long-term $HTP supply framework. Activation details will be disclosed when the mechanism becomes active.

What is the role of $HTP?

$HTP is designed to power tipping, community participation and future utility across the HappyTip ecosystem.
